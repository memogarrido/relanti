The Burbuja family

Turbo stays a round micro car with headlight eyes and bonnet brows. This page pushes that shape in three directions (size, age, equipment), then asks what Relanti could be next to him: a second car, your own car, or something else.

Part 1

Turbo, six ways

The same face on different bodies. All drawn at the same scale, so the small one really is small.

Part 2

What is Relanti?

If the app is named after a second character, the pair should be a contrast that teaches something. Three contrasts, each readable both ways round.

Part 3

Or Relanti is your car

The idea I like most. Turbo is the veteran classic instructor. Relanti is the car you are given on day one: new, simple, nothing fitted. As your rango grows, Turbo fits one piece of kit, just like Volantón's owlet got a uniform piece at each rango. You pick its colour, as kids picked their owlet's plumage.

One piece per rango, never taken away. Share cards show the car without the rango kit, so a shared picture never tells your level.

Part 4

Other options

Relanti is the caravan

A little round caravan that Turbo tows. It is your Guantera on wheels: stickers on its sides, your collection inside. Charming for the road trip, and it keeps Turbo as the only face.

Relanti is the road

No second character. Relanti is the name of the road trip and the world (the Ruta Relanti), and Turbo is the only one who talks. Simplest to build and to explain.

The hare and the tortoise, inverted

Turbo is the slow, wise old car (the name is the joke) and Relanti is the twitchy little one who wants to rush. Turbo keeps telling him "a ralentí". Funny, but it puts the user's feelings in the sidekick.

Two instructors, two modes

Turbo leads study mode (tramos, trucos) and Relanti leads exam mode (simulacros), calm and strict like a real examiner. Clear, but two voices is more art and more copy to keep consistent.

My recommendation

Old Turbo, new Relanti. Turbo is the classic Burbuja instructor with chrome and an L sign, warm and wise. Relanti is your own new car, which starts simple and gets equipped as you progress. Together they cover old and new, simple and equipped, and they give the app a clear growth story that ends when you pass: Turbo takes off his L sign, and Relanti is ready for your first real road trip.

Sketches drawn in code to compare directions; the final art would be illustrated and rigged in layers. All shapes are invented, not based on a real model.
